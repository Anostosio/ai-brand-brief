Privacy in Brand Brief Studio

Brand Brief Studio is designed as a static, browser-local product. Questionnaire content is processed on your device and is not sent to a Brand Brief Studio backend or an external AI provider.

Pre-launch review. The operator contact and target static host are recorded below. Final publication still requires verification of the live analytics settings and network behaviour.

1. Operator and contact

The operator of Brand Brief Studio is an individual who owns and operates the Anostosio° / Product Lab project.

Personal-data and privacy requests: Anostosio@yandex.ru.

No IP/INN/OGRN, self-employed status or other registration details are stated because the operator has not represented herself as an individual entrepreneur or self-employed person for this product.

2. Questionnaire and generated brief

The questionnaire is processed by JavaScript running in your browser. Pressing Build working brief does not upload questionnaire fields to a Brand Brief Studio server and does not call Groq, YandexGPT or another remote model.

The result is produced by a deterministic evidence-aware rules engine bundled with the site. It uses only the fields you entered, marks hypotheses and validation gaps, and does not claim to perform market research.

3. Local browser storage

The questionnaire draft and recent briefs are stored through the browser Local Storage API. Up to eight recent briefs are retained per interface language. A history entry can contain questionnaire input, generated output, evidence metadata, alternative directions and local timestamps.

There is no Brand Brief Studio account and no application database synchronizing these records. They remain on the current device/browser until you delete them, clear site data or the browser removes them.

The Delete local data action removes Brand Brief Studio draft/history keys from this browser. JSON/PDF files exported elsewhere are outside the site's control.

4. Static hosting and technical request data

The target deployment is the public SourceCraft Sites repository Anostosio Product Lab / ai-brand-brief, published at https://anostosio-product-lab.sourcecraft.site/ai-brand-brief/.

Delivering any website necessarily exposes ordinary request metadata such as IP address, request headers and timestamps to the hosting infrastructure. Brand Brief Studio has no application backend that receives questionnaire bodies and does not use visitor IP addresses for product-level rate limiting.

The site does not promise a specific infrastructure-log retention period until that behaviour is verified against the live SourceCraft service and its current terms/settings.

5. Yandex Metrica

Yandex Metrica is optional. Its tag is loaded only after you choose Allow analytics. If you decline, the application sets the counter-disable flag and does not initialize the counter.

Session Replay / Webvisor is disabled in the site code. The questionnaire is not intentionally recorded through session replay. You can reopen Analytics settings and withdraw your choice. On withdrawal the application disables further counter activity and makes a best-effort deletion of accessible first-party Metrica cookies and browser-storage identifiers.

Withdrawal affects future analytics processing; it cannot retroactively remove events already sent. The operator must also keep the actual Metrica dashboard configuration aligned with this notice.

7. External services

The target questionnaire flow has no remote AI provider. Google Fonts is removed from the target runtime; typography uses local/system assets until licensed font files are self-hosted. The portfolio link opens only after you choose it. Yandex Metrica is the only optional analytics dependency and is consent-gated.

8. Your choices

You can use the brief builder without enabling analytics. You can remove locally stored product data through the interface and clear all site data in your browser at any time.

For privacy or personal-data questions relating to hosting or optional analytics, contact Anostosio@yandex.ru.

9. Production status

This notice describes the zero-cost browser-local SourceCraft target. The legacy Vercel/Groq deployment must not be represented by this notice after cutover planning begins. Final cutover requires a live network check and verification of the Metrica dashboard configuration.
